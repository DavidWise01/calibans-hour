Caliban's Hour1994 · standalone · the monster's side

A short, literary fantasy: twenty years after The Tempest, Caliban comes to Italy, fully grown, to hold an aging Miranda captive and force his side of the story to be heard before he takes his revenge. A revisionist sequel to Shakespeare told from the monster's point of view, with notes of Beauty and the Beast and Robinson Crusoe.
